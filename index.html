Friday Content Publisher

A personal automation tool that publishes operator-approved short videos to the operator's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

How it works

  1. Connect. The operator authorizes the app once through TikTok Login Kit, granting it permission to read basic profile info and post videos.
  2. Approve. Every video is prepared with its title, description, and cover image, and nothing is uploaded until the operator explicitly approves that specific video.
  3. Publish. Approved videos are posted directly to the operator's own TikTok account using TikTok's Content Posting API with Direct Post.

What it does and doesn't do

  • Publishes only to the operator's own TikTok account. It cannot post to anyone else's account.
  • Every upload requires explicit operator approval. There is no automatic or unattended posting.
  • Uses only TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. No scraping, no unofficial endpoints.
  • Collects no analytics, serves no ads, and shares data with no third parties beyond transmitting the approved video to TikTok itself.
  • Built and operated by Jim Higgins as a personal productivity tool. It is not offered as a public service.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sign up to use Friday Content Publisher?
No. It is a personal tool built for a single operator's own TikTok account. There are no user accounts, no sign-up flow, and no public offering.
What data does the app handle?
TikTok OAuth tokens, basic profile info from Login Kit, and the video files plus titles, descriptions, and cover images the operator approves. See the Privacy Policy for the full details.
How do I request data deletion?
Email tiktok@higgins.ws from the account associated with the app and all stored credentials and data for that authorization will be deleted.
Who do I contact with questions?
Jim Higgins, the app's developer and operator, at tiktok@higgins.ws.
